This bombilla (Filtro de Resorte) has a spring style filter wrapped around the bottom. It actually 
expands to fit against the sides of the mate, which does a great job of blocking any loose yerba from 
getting through. It's definitely a step up in terms of filtration compared to my first spoon bombilla. 
If you hate getting dust in your sips, this is the one to get.

**Best for**: Fine cut yerba. The spring filter is super effective at catching those tiny particles, 
making it a top tier choice if you prefer a more powdery blend.

**Cleaning tip**: Just slide the spring off.
