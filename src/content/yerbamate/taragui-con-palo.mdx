**Taragüí Con Palo** totally lives up to its reputation as the golden mean and a true 
classic down in Argentina, it's pretty much a staple in every single household for a reason. 
If you've ever tried the **Sin Palo** version and felt like it was just too intense, 
this one is so much easier to handle, at least for me. It still gives you that same solid, 
energizing kick that makes it a great, healthy swap for coffee, but mixing in those 
stems (palo) really mellows out the whole experience. On top of that, it doesn't create 
a ton of foam when you pour the water, which makes the whole prep super clean and easy, 
even if I prefer to have foam. Honestly, for me, it strikes the perfect balance, 
making it way more pleasant to sip on day after day.

Honestly, for me, this is the ideal classic mate when I want enough strong energy to 
push through the day, but without quite such an intense hit. If I compare it to 
**Mañanita**, honestly, **Mañanita** is my favorite and I'd drink it every single day, 
**Taragüí Con Palo** is definitely a bit more bitter, but is still has its own moments where it truly hits that sweet 
spot of the golden mean.


### Tereré

I tried it with a squeeze of lemon, and even though it keeps that true classic 
taste and still doesn't create a lot of foam, it's still a really tasty combination. 
It's a true pleasure to drink it this way!
